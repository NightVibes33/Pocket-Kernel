Privacy at PocketKernel

Effective August 4, 2026

Plain-language summary: PocketKernel does not run a cloud language model, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites. Planning happens with Apple’s on-device model. Connected services receive only the information required to perform an action you approve or an automation you save.

Information stored on your device

Your chat history, activity history, onboarding preference, and an app-specific device identifier are stored in PocketKernel’s private app container or Keychain.

Information stored by the automation service

To keep service connections and scheduled automations working, PocketKernel stores an app-specific account identifier, encrypted service authorization credentials, automation titles, schedules, and deterministic action details. Ordinary chat conversations are not uploaded as model prompts.

Connected services

When you connect Google, Slack, Discord, Reddit, Notion, or another supported service, sign-in occurs on that service’s official authorization page. PocketKernel never receives your service password. You can disconnect a service in the app.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate your PocketKernel session, execute approved actions, run saved automations, keep the service secure, and provide support. PocketKernel does not use your information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Connected-service credentials and automations remain until you disconnect the service, delete an automation, or use “Delete my PocketKernel data” in the app. Local chat and activity history can also be cleared in the app.

Security

Service credentials are encrypted before storage. Network traffic uses HTTPS. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so access is limited to the minimum required for the requested feature.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the PocketKernel support page.
