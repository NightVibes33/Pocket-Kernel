PocketKernel Support

Apple Intelligence is not ready

Open Settings, make sure Apple Intelligence is enabled, connect to Wi‑Fi and power, and allow the on-device model to finish downloading.

A connection will not complete

Try connecting again from PocketKernel → You → Connections. Sign in on the service’s official page and approve the requested access. Disconnecting and reconnecting can refresh expired authorization.

An automation did not run

Check that the automation is Active, the required service remains connected, and the scheduled time has passed. Open Activity for the latest result.

Delete your data

Open PocketKernel → You → Delete my PocketKernel data. This removes connected services, cloud automations, and local history.

For additional help, open an issue in the PocketKernel GitHub repository or contact the developer through the App Store support contact.
